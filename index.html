FAQ
Common questions
How do I cancel my order? ↓
You can cancel your order within 24 hours by going to My Orders section and clicking Cancel Order next to your order.
What is your refund policy? ↓
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive your returned item. Original payment method will be credited.
How can I track my delivery? ↓
Go to My Orders in your account and click Track Order. You'll see real-time updates on your delivery status.
How do I contact support? ↓
You can chat with our AI assistant anytime using the chat button, or email us at support@shopease.com during business hours.
